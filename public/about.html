About HoneyCat3D

Honeycat Studios is a husband & wife team — he prints, she paints. Every piece starts as a 3D print, then gets hand-finished with care before it ever reaches your shelf.

Our cat Zuko likes to watch and boss us around. He has no printing or painting skills of his own, but he's an excellent supervisor.

We create collector-grade resin statues for gamers, hobbyists, and display enthusiasts who want something a little more special than what's on the shelf.

Follow along on Instagram @honey.cat.studios for behind-the-scenes prints, paint progress, and the occasional Zuko cameo.
